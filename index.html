MY SCHOOL • AMU

Minto Circle

S.T.S. School • Aligarh Muslim University

1875 School founded as Madarsatul Uloom
1909 Construction of the present Minto Circle building began

Minto Circle is the historic campus of S.T.S. School, Aligarh Muslim University. Its story goes back to the educational movement associated with Sir Syed Ahmad Khan.

Teaching began in 1875 with eleven students. Over time, the institution became an important part of the educational tradition that developed around Aligarh Muslim University.

The present building, whose construction began in 1909, gives Minto Circle its distinctive historic character. The school has continued its tradition of academic development, discipline and national character.
